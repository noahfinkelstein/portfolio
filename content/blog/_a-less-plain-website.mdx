Five weeks ago I wrote that nothing on this site moves. That post is still up,
one entry below this one, and it is now wrong.

This version is dark by default. A switcher in the nav offers Night, Cyan,
Terminal, and Paper, and Paper is the old white page, more or less, for anyone
who preferred it. Above the fold there is a wireframe torus knot drawn with
three.js, the same shape that was spinning on the very first version of this
site, the one I was rude about in the last post. My name decodes in with a
scramble effect. Under the hero, three giant lines of type slide sideways as
you scroll: Mathematics–CS, Machine Learning, Co-Founder. Project cards play
real screen recordings of CourseTrees, BrownSync, and brown3d instead of
mock-ups. The Experience section tosses the logos of the tools I use around a
small physics box, courtesy of matter-js.

I stand by the reasoning in the last post. The template site was slow and said
things about me that were not true, and a white page fixed both. It also hid
something. Most of what I build moves: a course catalog laid out by a force
simulation, a map of College Hill where the buildings light up when a class is
in session, a Providence you can walk around in a browser. A site with none of
that on it was accurate about my email address and not much else. This is a
personal site, which means it is allowed to show what I like doing, and what I
like doing is making things move.

Some of the plainness survived. Everything is in the server-rendered HTML, so
the page reads fine with JavaScript off. If your system asks for reduced
motion, there is no loader, no scramble, the knot holds still, and nothing
autoplays. The /experience page prints as a black-on-white CV. It is still
Next.js and plain CSS with no UI framework.

I do not promise this is the last version. The first one had too much on it,
the second had too little, and I would like to think the third has the right
amount, which is also what I thought about the second.
