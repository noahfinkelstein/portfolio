There's a feeling I get when a proof finally clicks that is *exactly* the same
as the feeling when a stubborn bug finally reveals itself. I don't think that's
a coincidence.

## Both are search problems

Whether I'm staring at a physics problem set or a stack trace, the move is the
same: shrink the space of possibilities until the answer has nowhere left to
hide.

1. State precisely what you're trying to show.
2. Find the smallest case that still fails (or still needs proving).
3. Attack that.
