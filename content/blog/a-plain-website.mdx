The last version of this site was in dark mode, with a particle field drifting
behind my name and a three-dimensional object that spun when you scrolled. The
headline cycled through nine things I supposedly did, full stack development
and aerospace engineering among them. It came from a template and looked like
one, and it took a while to load a page whose entire useful content was my
email address.

This one is a white page. The text is set in Newsreader and the code in IBM
Plex Mono, with the dates in a column down the left. Nothing moves. If you came
here to find out what I have worked on, that is now the first thing on the
screen, with nothing in front of it.

I kept the blog because I wanted somewhere to put things that are too long for
a text message and too small for a paper, a proof I liked or a bug that took
three days to find. This is the first post, and for now the only one.
